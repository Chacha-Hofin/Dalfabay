Our Story

Fashion that remembers where it's been.

Every piece in our collection has lived a life before yours. We believe clothing carries memory — the parties attended, the streets walked, the decades witnessed. Dalfabay was founded to honor that history, giving extraordinary vintage pieces a second chapter with people who truly appreciate them.
